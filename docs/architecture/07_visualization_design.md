# Visualization Design

## 1. Overview

The visualization layer represents the final stage of the Lakehouse architecture, providing users with direct access to curated analytical datasets.

Apache Superset has been selected as the Business Intelligence platform due to its Open Source nature, web-based interface, and seamless integration with Spark SQL.

The visualization layer enables users to explore meteorological and energy data through interactive dashboards, charts, filters, and analytical reports without requiring direct access to the underlying Lakehouse infrastructure.

Only datasets published in the Gold layer are exposed for analytical consumption, ensuring that all visualizations are based on validated and business-ready data.

## 2. Business Intelligence Architecture

The Business Intelligence layer provides analytical access to the curated datasets generated by the Lakehouse.

Apache Superset connects to Spark SQL to execute analytical queries against the Gold layer without directly accessing raw or intermediate datasets. This architecture ensures that all reports and dashboards are built on validated, standardized, and business-ready data.

The Business Intelligence architecture follows a clear separation of responsibilities:

- Apache Spark prepares the analytical datasets.
- Spark SQL provides the query interface.
- Apache Superset delivers data visualization and interactive analysis.

This separation simplifies maintenance, improves data consistency, and ensures that visualization remains independent from data processing and storage.

## 3. Dashboard Strategy

The visualization layer is designed to provide a set of dashboards that support the exploration and analysis of meteorological and energy data.

Rather than presenting raw datasets, dashboards expose curated information through interactive visualizations that enable users to identify trends, compare regions, and explore relationships between different variables.

The dashboards are organized according to the main analytical domains of the project:

- **Meteorological Dashboard**
  
  Focused on weather variables such as temperature, precipitation, wind speed, and other available meteorological indicators.

- **Energy Dashboard**
  
  Focused on electricity generation, energy demand, and electricity market prices.

- **Integrated Analysis Dashboard**
  
  Combines meteorological and energy datasets to explore potential relationships between weather conditions and the behaviour of the electrical system.

Each dashboard provides interactive filtering capabilities and supports exploratory analysis without requiring knowledge of the underlying data model.

## 4. Key Performance Indicators

The visualization layer provides a collection of Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) that summarize the most relevant meteorological and energy information available in the Lakehouse.

KPIs are calculated from the curated datasets stored in the Gold layer and provide a concise overview of the system without requiring users to explore the underlying datasets.

The platform is designed to support indicators related to:

- Meteorological conditions.
- Electricity generation.
- Electricity demand.
- Electricity market prices.
- Temporal evolution of the analyzed variables.
- Comparative analysis between Autonomous Communities.

The final set of KPIs will be defined during the implementation phase according to the analytical capabilities provided by the selected public APIs.

## 5. Data Exploration

The visualization layer supports interactive data exploration, allowing users to analyze the curated datasets from multiple perspectives without requiring direct access to the underlying data model.

Apache Superset provides a flexible environment where users can filter, compare, and explore meteorological and energy data through interactive visualizations and analytical dashboards.

The platform supports exploratory analysis by enabling users to:

- Filter data by time period.
- Compare different Autonomous Communities.
- Analyze historical trends.
- Explore relationships between meteorological and energy variables.
- Navigate through different levels of analytical detail.

This interactive approach allows users to identify patterns, trends, and correlations while maintaining a simple and intuitive user experience.

## 6. Design Principles

The visualization layer has been designed according to the following principles:

- **Business-ready data**
  
  All dashboards and visualizations are based exclusively on curated datasets from the Gold layer.

- **Consistency**
  
  All reports use the same analytical datasets, ensuring that users always work with consistent and validated information.

- **Interactivity**
  
  Dashboards support filtering and exploration, allowing users to analyze the data from different perspectives.

- **Usability**
  
  Visualizations are designed to present complex analytical information in a clear, intuitive, and accessible manner.

- **Scalability**
  
  New dashboards, KPIs, and analytical views can be added without modifying the underlying Lakehouse architecture.

- **Separation of responsibilities**
  
  The visualization layer is responsible only for presenting information, while data ingestion, processing, storage, and orchestration remain independent components of the platform.
